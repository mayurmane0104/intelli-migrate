# Sample data - Amazon Quick

**Source:** [https://docs.aws.amazon.com/quick/latest/userguide/sample-data.html](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/quick/latest/userguide/sample-data.html)

---

To get a first look at how Amazon Quick Sight works, you can explore Amazon Quick Sight using the following sample data:

- 
					[Business overview data](samples/business-overview.csv.zip)(revenue)
- 
					[People overview data](samples/people-overview.csv.zip)(human resources)
- 
					[Web and social media analytics data](samples/web-and-social-analytics.csv.zip)(marketing)

Also, a variety of datasets are available free online that you can use with
				Amazon Quick Sight, for example the [AWS
					public datasets](https://aws.amazon.com/public-datasets/). These datasets come in a variety of formats.
