# Managing identities - Amazon Quick

**Source:** [https://docs.aws.amazon.com/quick/latest/userguide/qbs-identity.html](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/quick/latest/userguide/qbs-identity.html)

---

Identity management in Amazon Quick involves controlling who can access your Quick resources and what actions they can perform. This section covers the essential tasks for managing user identities, including adding and removing users, organizing them into groups for easier permission management, and transitioning between different identity management systems. Whether you're using IAM Identity Center, federated identity providers, or other authentication methods, these topics will help you establish proper access controls and maintain security for your Quick environment.

Before managing user access, it's important to understand how Amazon Quick
            subscription names map to user roles. For information about subscription types and their
            capabilities, see [Understanding Amazon Quick subscriptions and
            roles](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/quicksuite/latest/userguide/user-types.html).
