# Renaming bookmarks in Amazon Quick Sight - Amazon Quick

**Source:** [https://docs.aws.amazon.com/quick/latest/userguide/dashboard-bookmarks-rename.html](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/quick/latest/userguide/dashboard-bookmarks-rename.html)

---

Use the following procedure to rename a bookmark.

###### To rename a bookmark

- 
                    In a published dashboard, choose the bookmark icon at upper right to open the **Bookmarks**pane.
- 
                    In the **Bookmarks**pane, choose the context menu (the three vertical dots) for the bookmark that you want to rename, and then choose**rename**.
- 
                    In the **Rename bookmark**pane, enter a name for the bookmark, and then choose**Save**.
