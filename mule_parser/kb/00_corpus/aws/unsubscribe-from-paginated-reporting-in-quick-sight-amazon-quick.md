# Unsubscribe from paginated reporting in Quick Sight - Amazon Quick

**Source:** [https://docs.aws.amazon.com/quick/latest/userguide/qs-reports-getting-started-unsubscribe.html](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/quick/latest/userguide/qs-reports-getting-started-unsubscribe.html)

---

You can unsubscribe from Quick Sight pixel perfect reporting at any time. Once you unsubscribe from pixel perfect reporting, you will lose the ability to create and schedule pixel perfect reports in Quick Sight. You are still able to access your existing paginated reports, but you won't be able to make changes or schedule new reports.

###### To unsubscribe from pixel perfect reporting in Amazon Quick Sight

- 
                From any page in Quick, choose your user name in the upper right, and choose **Manage Quick**.
- 
                Choose **Manage subscriptions**on the left.
- 
                On the **Manage subscriptions**page, locate the**Pixel-Perfect Reports**section and choose**Manage**.
- 
                Scroll down to your chosen subscription plan and choose **Cancel subscription**.
