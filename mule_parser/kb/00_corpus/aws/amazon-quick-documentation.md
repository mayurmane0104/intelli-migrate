# Amazon Quick Documentation

Amazon Quick is a comprehensive, generative AI-powered business intelligence platform that makes it
    easy to analyze data, create visualizations, automate workflows, and collaborate across your organization. The service
    combines traditional business intelligence capabilities with modern AI assistance, requiring no machine learning expertise to
    use. You can connect to diverse data sources, create interactive dashboards, build intelligent automations, and
    get immediate insights through natural language conversations with AI agents.
