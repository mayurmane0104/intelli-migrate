# Monitoring upgrades - Amazon Quick

**Source:** [https://docs.aws.amazon.com/quick/latest/userguide/monitoring-upgrades.html](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/quick/latest/userguide/monitoring-upgrades.html)

---

| Applies
                    to:Enterprise Edition | 

| Intended audience: System administrators and Amazon Quick administrators | 

Administrators can monitor user upgrades through:

- 
            **CloudTrail logs**— Provides audit trails of all upgrade requests and approvals.

## Billing considerations for administrators

        
        - 
                Users are billed for their highest license tier within each billing period. 
- 
                **Prorated Billing:**When you upgrade a user subscription in Amazon QuickSuite, billing is prorated for the month of the upgrade. This means:If you upgrade from BI Reader ($3/month) to Professional ($20/month), you're billed at the $20/month rate from the time of upgrade until the end of that billing month. If you then upgrade again from Professional ($20/month) to Enterprise ($40/month) within the same month, you're billed at the $40/month rate from that second upgrade time until the end of the month.
