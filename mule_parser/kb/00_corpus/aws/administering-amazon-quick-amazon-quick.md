# Administering Amazon Quick - Amazon Quick

**Source:** [https://docs.aws.amazon.com/quick/latest/userguide/administering-quick.html](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/quick/latest/userguide/administering-quick.html)

---

The administration experience for Amazon Quick depends on how your account was created. Choose the guide that matches your account type:

- 
            **Free and Plus accounts**– For accounts created at[quick.aws.com](https://quick.aws.com). See[Administering Amazon Quick (Free/Plus)](./standalone-admin-guide.html).
- 
            **Console accounts**– For accounts provisioned through the AWS Management Console, including IAM Identity Center configuration, SPICE capacity management, and integration with other AWS services. See[Administration for Amazon Quick](./qsysadmin.html).
