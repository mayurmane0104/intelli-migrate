# Printing a dashboard or analysis - Amazon Quick

**Source:** [https://docs.aws.amazon.com/quick/latest/userguide/printing1.html](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/quick/latest/userguide/printing1.html)

---

You can print a dashboard or an analysis in Amazon Quick Sight.

Use the following procedure to print.

- 
				Open the dashboard or the analysis that you want to print. 
- 
				Choose the **Print**icon at top right.
- 
				On the **Prepare for printing**screen, choose the paper size and orientation that you want to use.
- 
				Choose **Go to Preview**.
- 
				Do one of the following: - 
						To proceed to printing, choose **Print**to open your operating system's print dialog.
- 
						To make changes to the paper size or orientation, choose **Configure**.
 
- 
						
- 
				To exit the preview screen, choose **Exit preview**.
