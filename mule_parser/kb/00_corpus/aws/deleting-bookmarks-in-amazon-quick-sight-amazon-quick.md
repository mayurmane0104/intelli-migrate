# Deleting bookmarks in Amazon Quick Sight - Amazon Quick

**Source:** [https://docs.aws.amazon.com/quick/latest/userguide/dashboard-bookmarks-delete.html](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/quick/latest/userguide/dashboard-bookmarks-delete.html)

---

Use the following procedure to delete a bookmark.

###### To delete a bookmark

- 
                    In a published dashboard, choose the bookmark icon at upper right to open the **Bookmarks**pane.
- 
                    In the **Bookmarks**pane, choose the context menu (the three vertical dots) for the bookmark that you want to delete, and then choose**Delete**.
- 
                    In the **Delete Bookmark**pane that opens, choose**Yes, Delete Bookmark**.
