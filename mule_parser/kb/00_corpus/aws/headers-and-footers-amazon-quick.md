# Headers and footers - Amazon Quick

**Source:** [https://docs.aws.amazon.com/quick/latest/userguide/qs-reports-add-delete-headers-footers.html](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/quick/latest/userguide/qs-reports-add-delete-headers-footers.html)

---

*Headers* and *Footers* are optional
                    special sections located at the top and bottom of a pixel perfect report. Headers
                    and footers are commonly used to display basic information like the date the
                    report was created or the page number. You can interact with headers and footers
                    the same way you interact with a regular section in a report.

By default, every report in Amazon Quick Sight has a header and a footer. To remove the header or footer from your report, use the following procedure.

###### To remove a header or footer from a pixel perfect report

- 
                        In your pixel perfect report, navigate to the header or footer that you want to delete and open the **On-section**.
- 
                        Choose **Delete**.

When you delete a header or footer from your report, you are deleting the header or footer from every page of the report. You can't have a header or footer on some pages but not others.

If you have removed the header or footer from your report but want them to be visible again, use the following procedure.

###### To add a header or footer to a pixel perfect report

- 
                        Navigate to the pixel perfect report that you want to add a header or footer to and choose **Insert**from the top menu.
- 
                        Choose **Add header**or**Add footer**.
