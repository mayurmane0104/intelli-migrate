# Signing in to a Amazon Quick Free or Plus account - Amazon Quick

**Source:** [https://docs.aws.amazon.com/quick/latest/userguide/signing-in-standalone.html](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/quick/latest/userguide/signing-in-standalone.html)

---

If you created your Amazon Quick account at [quick.aws.com](https://quick.aws.com), follow these steps to sign in.

###### To sign in to a Amazon Quick Free or Plus account

- 
                Navigate to [https://quick.aws.com](https://quick.aws.com).
- 
                Choose **Sign in**.
- 
                Enter the email address associated with your account. 
- 
                Enter your password. 
- 
                Choose **Sign in**.

If you have multiple Amazon Quick accounts associated with your email, you may be prompted to select which account to access. Choose the account you want to sign in to.

###### Tip

You can also sign in using the social login option you used during signup (Google, Apple, GitHub, or Amazon) if you linked your account during registration.

###### Note

If you signed up through the AWS Management Console instead, see the next section for console sign-in instructions.
