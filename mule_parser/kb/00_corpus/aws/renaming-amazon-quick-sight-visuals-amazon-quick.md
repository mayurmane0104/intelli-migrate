# Renaming Amazon Quick Sight visuals - Amazon Quick

**Source:** [https://docs.aws.amazon.com/quick/latest/userguide/renaming-a-visual.html](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/quick/latest/userguide/renaming-a-visual.html)

---

Use the following procedure to rename a visual.

###### To rename a visual

- 
                    On the analysis page, choose the visual that you want to rename. 
- 
                    Select the visual name at the top left of the visual and enter a new name. 
- 
                    Press `Enter`or click outside of the visual name field to save the new name.
