# Alert Permissions - Amazon Quick

**Source:** [https://docs.aws.amazon.com/quick/latest/userguide/threshold-alerts-permissions.html](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/quick/latest/userguide/threshold-alerts-permissions.html)

---

If you're an administrator, you can control who in your organization can set
				threshold alerts in Quick Sight by creating a custom permissions policy. To set
				custom permissions in Quick, choose your user name at the upper-right
				corner of any Quick page, choose **Manage
					Quick**, and then choose **Custom
					permissions**.
