# Updating bookmarks in Amazon Quick Sight - Amazon Quick

**Source:** [https://docs.aws.amazon.com/quick/latest/userguide/dashboard-bookmarks-update.html](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/quick/latest/userguide/dashboard-bookmarks-update.html)

---

At any time, you can change a bookmark dashboard view and update the bookmark to always reflect those changes.

###### To update a bookmark

- 
                    Open the published dashboard and make needed changes to the filters or parameters, or select a sheet. 
- 
                    Choose the bookmark icon at upper right. 
- 
                    In the **Bookmarks**pane that opens, choose the context menu (the three vertical dots) for the bookmark that you want to update, and then choose**Update**.A message appears, confirming the update.
