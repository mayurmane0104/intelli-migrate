# Choosing a layout - Amazon Quick

**Source:** [https://docs.aws.amazon.com/quick/latest/userguide/choosing-a-layout.html](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/quick/latest/userguide/choosing-a-layout.html)

---

###### To change a dashboard's layout

- 
	Open the [Quick console](https://quicksight.aws.amazon.com/).
- 
                    From the Quick homepage, choose **Analyses**, and then choose the analysis that you want to change.
- 
                    On the analysis page, choose **Edit**and then choose**Analysis Settings**.
- 
                    Expand **Sheet layout**and choose the layout that you want to use.
- 
                    When finished, choose **Apply**.
