# Removing datasets from a Amazon Quick Sight topic - Amazon Quick

**Source:** [https://docs.aws.amazon.com/quick/latest/userguide/topics-data-remove.html](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/quick/latest/userguide/topics-data-remove.html)

---

You can remove datasets from a topic. Removing datasets from a topic doesn't delete them from Quick Sight.

Use the following procedure to remove a dataset from a topic.

###### To remove a dataset from a topic

- 
                    Open the topic that you want to change. 
- 
                    On the **Summary**page, choose**Data**. Then, under**Datasets**, choose the dataset menu (the three dots) at right, and then choose**Remove from topic**.
- 
                    On the **Are you sure you want to delete?**page that opens, choose**Delete**to remove the dataset from the topic. Choose**Cancel**if you don't want to remove the dataset from the topic.
