## Show me how

- 
Open the list of business groups in Access Management, on Anypoint Platform ( [US](https://anypoint.mulesoft.com/accounts/businessGroups/),[EU](https://eu1.anypoint.mulesoft.com/accounts/businessGroups)).To navigate to the business group list from the Anypoint Platform home page instead, open Access Management, and click **Business Groups**.
- 
In the business group list, click the name of your business group. 
- 
Click the **Settings**tab for your business group.
- 
Obtain the **Client ID**and**Client Secrets**values from the**Settings**tab in Access Manager.
