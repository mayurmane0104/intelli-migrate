# Sending Notifications to External Systems

[Advanced and Titanium tiers](/monitoring/#billing)

Anypoint Monitoring provides links to connectors on Anypoint Exchange that you can incorporate into a Mule app to deliver notifications to external systems.

![Tools in Anypoint Monitoring](_images/tools.png) 

- 
Log in to Anypoint Platform with a user account that has [permission to access Anypoint Monitoring content](am-permissions).
- 
Navigate to Anypoint Monitoring: Click **Monitoring**from the Anypoint Platform home page or from the**Runtimes**menu in the header.
- 
In the sidebar, click **Tools**.
- 
Review the connectors. You can hover over a connector link to learn more before navigating to Anypoint Exchange.
