## Viewing Usage and Billing

The method for viewing usage and handling billing varies based on the type of Automation purchase.

| Usage Type | Usage Visibility | Billing (monthly statements and overage) | 
|---|---|---|
| MuleSoft Automation Credits 3.0 | Digital Wallet (and optionally Anypoint Usage Reports if you have an Anypoint Platform org) | Digital Wallet |

## Monthly Account Summary Emails

Salesforce sends usage-based billing reports to every MuleSoft Composer, Intelligent Document Processing (IDP), and Robotic Process Automation (RPA) customer on the 10th day of each month. By default, Salesforce sends these emails to the account’s billing contact.

For more information about the reports, the list of recipients, and other details, see the [Monthly Account Summary Email FAQ](https://help.salesforce.com/s/articleView?id=000390885&type=1).
